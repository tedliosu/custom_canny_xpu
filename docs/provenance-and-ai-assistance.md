# Provenance and AI assistance

This document records material source and AI-assisted influences identified so
far. It separates externally derived algorithms from AI-assisted code, design
ideas, benchmark work, and maintainer decisions. It is a maintained inventory,
not a claim that every historical influence has been reconstructed.

## Third-party algorithm and source provenance

The high-level CCL decomposition of double hysteresis is derived from
scikit-image-style behavior: label the low-threshold mask, identify components
that contain a high-threshold pixel, and retain those components. The existing
helper was adapted from the then-current scikit-image `main` branch and was in
use by May 16, 2025. The exact upstream commit was not recorded and is treated
as irrecoverable unless new primary evidence appears. This algorithm must not
be described as invented by this project or by an AI assistant.

The planned refined GPU CCL derives from CuPy 14.1.x-era upstream source and is
expected to vendor the required templated traits with the derived kernels. Its
exact revision, adaptation, attribution, and license terms must be recorded in
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` when integrated. AI-assisted organization or
optimization discussion does not replace those third-party obligations.

## AI-assisted design and optimization ideas

The following high-level ideas were suggested through ChatGPT-assisted design
discussion:

- Reorganize or fuse the hysteresis work that marks labels containing strong
  pixels with the later work that retains pixels belonging to marked labels.
  This is an optimization-structure suggestion that evolved from the
  scikit-image-style CCL decomposition; it is not provenance for the
  decomposition itself.
- Separate convolution interiors from boundary regions so the common interior
  path can avoid repeated boundary-condition work while border pixels are
  handled explicitly. This records influence on the optimization idea, not an
  assertion that ChatGPT invented convolution or boundary handling.
- Apply register blocking or strip-mining to the column-parallel vertical
  convolution path. The maintainer imposed the consequential constraint that
  the optimization allocate no intermediate arrays, steering the accepted
  design toward direct computation and register/local reuse rather than
  packing, a full-image transpose, or another materialized representation.

For the vertical-convolution work, the maintainer set implementation-shape
constraints and decisions, selected and tested blocking factors, benchmarked
full and isolated workloads, inspected compiler output and assembly, evaluated
rejected variants, and made the integration-direction and stopping decisions.
The mixed provenance is therefore a ChatGPT-assisted high-level optimization
suggestion shaped and accepted through maintainer constraints and engineering
work. Do not flatten it into either complete AI implementation or wholly
independent human invention.

These statements describe influence on reviewed prototypes and design
direction. They do not claim that every corresponding refined stage is already
integrated into the current package; the README and roadmap remain authoritative
for implementation status.

Known material benchmark assistance includes:

- the May 2026 suspect-operator microbenchmark, fully generated by ChatGPT from
  a maintainer prompt;

  mostly ChatGPT-generated over several turns, with the final operator-class

  each generated by ChatGPT in one pass;

  possible later ChatGPT corrections; and
- the Kornia and OpenCV measurement harnesses, largely Gemini-generated with
  possible ChatGPT involvement.

The maintainer supplied the research questions, ran and checked experiments,
identified invalid or incomplete interpretations, changed implementations in
response to evidence, and decided which findings and scope boundaries to
retain. Detailed numerical context and irrecoverable authorship uncertainty
remain in the thematic experiment and historical notes rather than being
duplicated here.

## Maintainer ownership and acceptance

Architecture, public semantics, interfaces, invariants, support boundaries,
scope, testing, profiling, rewriting, benchmark acceptance, and final
integration remain human-owned. AI-generated or AI-suggested material is
review input, not an authority that overrides the maintainer's engineering
judgment. Suggestions may be tested, rejected, modified, or absorbed into a
larger maintainer-directed design.

All repository code and documentation have been drafted with some level of
ChatGPT-family assistance circa 2025–2026. Disclosure does not prove
originality, complete provenance, correctness, or license compliance. Code
copied, translated, or closely adapted from an external project still requires
source, revision, adaptation, and license review independently of AI
involvement.

## Inventory completeness

The items above are the material AI-assisted contributions identified and
documented so far; the inventory may not be exhaustive. Additional ideas,
snippets, refactors, benchmark designs, methodological suggestions, or tested
hypotheses may have influenced the final work directly or indirectly. Some
material was later rewritten, and exact line-by-line attribution may no longer
be reconstructable from memory alone.

Add newly recognized material contributions during future provenance review.
This caveat is not permission to ignore suspected undocumented third-party
similarity or licensing concerns. Such reports remain welcome and should lead
to review and, where appropriate, correction, attribution, or replacement.
